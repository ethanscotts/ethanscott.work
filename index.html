Journey Through The Endless Castle

ONGOING | Progression · Fantasy · updates M,F

Sentenced to die after seeking revenge for his father's murder, Wulfric is given a second chance at life when his King discovers his hidden powers and commands him to climb The Endless Castle: A tower in the heart of an endless war. Compelled to complete this task before their rivals Wulfric strives to reunite with his lost brother. The fate of the world is at stake, will Wulfric and his men discover the source of the Castle's power? Or die trying?
